## Reading and maintenance protocol

- Before writing world/story content, read the relevant entries and their cited source files.
- Add agreed world facts in the same change as the story/content that introduces them. Include stable ID, RU/UK names, status, source, spoiler boundary, and conflicts.
- Status `confirmed-user` means explicitly approved by the author; `project-source` means present in the cited existing project data; `episode-draft` means newly authored scene material, not established world history; `unresolved` must not be promoted to canon silently.
- Latest explicit author corrections take priority. Preserve earlier aliases as searchable history. If an apparent correction may be a slip in dictation, record the conflict and use the established source pending confirmation.
- Do not infer a mint, resource deposit, political border, admission rule or family secret from artwork or an NPC's opinion.
- This ledger does not replace runnable game data. Update the consuming content explicitly and keep RU/UK together.
