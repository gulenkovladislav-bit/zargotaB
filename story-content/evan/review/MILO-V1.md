## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE trial square 1024x1024 fantasy RPG dialogue portrait of fictional Milo, a boy about twelve with Down syndrome as specified by the author. Image 1 is loose reference for facial appearance and short dark hair ONLY, not its age, modern clothing, background or exact identity. Image 2 is STYLE, FRAMING AND PALETTE ONLY; Milo must look distinctly different from that slender boy. A rounded youthful face, soft full cheeks, short straight dark hair brushed sideways, brown eyes, natural relaxed closed mouth, attentive calm expression. Respectful believable human child, not caricature or comedy. Head and shoulders, slight three-quarter turn looking toward the viewer's right, generous margin for circular portrait crop. Fully clothed in an ordinary modest medieval brown wool tunic with cream linen collar. Warm ochre brown golden painted textured background, soft amber light, hand-painted fantasy oil illustration matching image2. No modern elements, no labels, no frame, no hands, no exaggerated expression. This is only a neutral first design for author approval.
